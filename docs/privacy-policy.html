Privacy policy

WaveHelm privacy policy

This policy describes how the public Windows desktop edition of WaveHelm handles user data.

Core statement

WaveHelm is designed for local media playback on Windows. The application does not require a user account, and it is not designed as a cloud service or social platform.

WaveHelm does not intentionally collect, sell, or broker personal data from your local media library or your playback history.

Data stored locally

The application may store settings, playlists, favorites, logs, cache files, database files, and generated exports on the local device so that the desktop workflow can function correctly.

Typical application data is stored under a user-local Windows application-data location such as %APPDATA%\WaveHelm.

Media files and content

WaveHelm works with media files that you choose locally. Your files remain under your control. The app does not publish your library contents to the developer.

If you choose to open external web links from the project site or contact support by email, those actions are initiated by you and are handled by the relevant third-party service.

Support contact

If you contact the developer directly, your email provider and normal email metadata will apply to that message. Support correspondence is used only to respond to your request and related troubleshooting.

Contact: mvsoundsystem81@gmail.com

Policy updates

This policy may be updated if future versions of WaveHelm add new online capabilities or materially different data-handling behavior. The current project site URL should always be treated as the latest public version of this policy.
